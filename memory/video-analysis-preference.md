When analyzing videos or audio, the user prefers fast information extraction, key takeaways, and core summaries rather than exact second-by-second timestamps or heavy full-audio whisper transcription. Always prioritize metadata, direct subtitles, and fast 16kHz mono audio processing to prevent phone overheating.
